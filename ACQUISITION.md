# Acquisition

This repository — the **theworker02** technology laboratory site and its supporting systems — is **available for acquisition**, **for sale**, and **customizable**.

## What is for sale

- The site codebase (Next.js App Router laboratory, content schemas, motion system, demos, APIs)
- Branding and content adaptation for a buyer or partner
- Deployment and hosting handoff (production is deployed on **Netlify**)
- Scope customization: navigation, featured systems, research archive, writing, acquisition flows

Selected curated projects listed on the live site as acquisition-available may be discussed separately or as a package. See [`/acquire`](https://theworker02.netlify.app/acquire).

## Customization

Buyers can commission changes including:

- Identity (name, handle, typography, worlds, visual marks)
- Content corpus (projects, research, lab, writing)
- Hosting target (Netlify remains the documented production host; other targets by agreement)
- Feature scope (search, GitHub sync, contact delivery, demos)

## How to proceed

1. Review the live site: [https://theworker02.netlify.app](https://theworker02.netlify.app)
2. Open [`/acquire`](https://theworker02.netlify.app/acquire) for systems marked available
3. Contact with intent **Acquisition**: [https://theworker02.netlify.app/contact?intent=Acquisition](https://theworker02.netlify.app/contact?intent=Acquisition) or email `matthewlooney5@gmail.com`

Do not treat marketing copy on the site as a valuation. Serious discussions start from the code, the archive, and written intent.
